Request Account Deletion

At Tehnico Provider, we respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

To request the deletion of your account, please send us an email with the following details:

  1. Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Tehnico Provider
  2. Registered Email/Phone: The email or phone number you used to register
  3. Full Name: As registered on the account
  4. Reason (Optional): Help us improve by telling us why you're leaving
📧 Send Deletion Request

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of your request, we will permanently delete the following data:

  • Account credentials (username and password)
  • Personal profile information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Profile photo and uploaded images
  • Service listings and portfolio
  • Booking history and customer interactions
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Chat messages and notifications
  • Location data and saved addresses
  • App preferences and settings

Data That May Be Retained

Note: Certain information may be retained for legal, regulatory, or security compliance reasons:

  • Transaction Records: Retained for up to 7 years for tax and financial compliance
  • Legal Records: Information required by law (e.g., fraud prevention, dispute resolution)
  • Anonymized Data: Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics data may be retained

Processing Timeline

⏱ Response Time: We will acknowledge your request within 72 hours.
✅ Completion: Your account and data will be fully deleted within 30 days of verification, in compliance with applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA).

Verification Process

To protect the security of your account, we may need to verify your identity before processing the deletion request. We may ask for additional information to confirm you are the rightful account owner.

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is permanent and irreversible
  • You will lose access to all services, bookings, and history
  • Any active subscriptions should be cancelled separately before deletion
  • Pending payments or disputes must be resolved prior to deletion
